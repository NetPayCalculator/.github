# Net Pay Calculator

[Net Pay Calculator](https://netpaycalculator.app/) is a free US paycheck
and take-home pay calculator.

## Features

- 2025 and 2026 federal income tax
- 50 states + DC
- Social Security and Medicare
- State and local taxes
- 401(k), HSA and FSA deductions
- Weekly, biweekly, semimonthly and monthly pay
- Salary-to-hourly conversion
- Detailed paycheck breakdown

## Website

https://netpaycalculator.app/

## About

Built to make US paycheck calculations easier to understand by showing
how gross pay becomes take-home pay.
